Worksheet Exercise 2.

For this problem, we will consider a Ferris wheel whose total height is \(40\)m.
🔗
Recall that we are measuring distance travelled, \(d\text{,}\) relative to the point on the circle to the extreme right, and we are measuring the height, \(h\text{,}\) relative to the center of the Ferris wheel, so, for example, when \(d=0\)m, \(h=0\)m.
🔗

(a)

How high is the center of the Ferris wheel above the ground?
🔗
Answer.
\(20\)m.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The total height is \(40\)m, so the center is half that distance above the ground, which is \(20\)m.
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

What is the circumference of the Ferris wheel?
🔗
Answer.
\(\approx 125.6637\)m.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
We will call the radius of the Ferris wheel \(r\) and the circumference \(C\text{.}\) Since the total height is twice the radius, we have \(r=20\)m, and thus \(C=2\pi r=2\pi\cdot 20\text{m}\approx{}125.6637\)m.
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

What is the value of \(h\) when \(31.4159\)m?
🔗
Answer.
\(20\)m.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
When the car has travelled \(31.4159\text{m}=\frac{1}{4}\cdot{}125.6637\)m, it has travelled \(\frac{1}{4}\) of the circumference. Because we are measuring \(d\) from the point on the extreme right, this means that the Ferris wheel car is at its highest point. Since we are measuring \(h\) from the center of the Ferris wheel, that means that \(h=20\)m.
🔗
🔗
🔗

(d)

What is the value of \(h\) when \(d=62.8315\)m?
🔗
Answer.
\(0\)m.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
When the car has travelled \(62.8315\text{m}=\frac{1}{2}\cdot{}125.6637\)m, it has travelled \(\frac{1}{2}\) of the circumference. Because we are measuring \(d\) from the point on the extreme right, this means that the Ferris wheel car is at the extreme left. Since we are measuring \(h\) from the center of the Ferris wheel and we are back at the original height, that means that \(h=0\)m.
🔗
🔗
🔗

(e)

What is the value of \(h\) when \(d=94.2478\)m?
🔗
Answer.
\(-20\)m.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
When the car has travelled \(94.2478\text{m}=\frac{3}{4}\cdot{}125.6637\)m, it has travelled \(\frac{3}{4}\) of the circumference. Because we are measuring \(d\) from the point on the extreme right, this means that the Ferris wheel car is at the bottom of its path, so it is \(20\)m below the center, and therefore \(h=-20\)m.
