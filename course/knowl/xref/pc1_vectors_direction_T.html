Task 5.1.3.d.

Use geometry and your knowledge of the unit circle to find a vector of length \(1\) pointing in the direction of \(\vec{F}_{T}\text{.}\) Call this vector \(\vec{u}_{T}\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
If we again put the origin at the pendulum bob, then the angle from the positive \(x\)-axis to the \(\vec{F}_{T}\) is \(-\pi/2+\pi/6=-\pi/3\text{,}\) so the point on the unit circle at that angle would be
\begin{align*} \vec{u}_{T}&=\left[\begin{array}{r}\cos\left(\frac{-\pi}{3}\right)\\[1.5ex]\sin\left(\frac{-\pi}{3}\right)\end{array}\right]\\ &=\left[\begin{array}{r}\frac{1}{2}\\[1.5ex]\frac{-\sqrt{3}}{2}\end{array}\right] \end{align*}
