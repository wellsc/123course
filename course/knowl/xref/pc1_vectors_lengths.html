Task 5.1.3.b.

Draw a copy of \(\vec{F}_{T}\) with its tail at the head of \(\vec{F}_{R}\) to form a right triangle and use trigonometry to find the lengths of \(\vec{F}_{T}\) and \(\vec{F}_{R}\text{.}\) Call these lengths \(\ell_{T}\) and \(\ell_{R}\text{.}\)
🔗
Solution.
We can see that \(\frac{\ell_{R}}{\cos(\theta_{2})}=\frac{32}{1}\text{,}\) so \(\ell_{R}=32\cos\left(\pi/3\right)=32\cdot\frac{1}{2}=16\text{,}\) and similarly, \(\frac{\ell_{T}}{\sin(\theta_{2})}=\frac{32}{1}\text{,}\) so \(\ell_{T}=32\sin\left(\pi/3\right)=32\sqrt{3}/2=16\sqrt{3}\approx 27.7128\text{.}\)
