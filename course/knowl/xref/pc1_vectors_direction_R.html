Task 5.1.3.c.

Use your knowledge of the unit circle to find a vector of length \(1\) pointing in the direction of \(\vec{F}_{R}\text{.}\) Call this vector \(\vec{u}_{R}\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
You can draw the unit circle with its center at the pendulum bob.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
If we put the origin at the pendulum bob, then the angle from the positive \(x\)-axis to the \(\vec{F}_{R}\) is \(\pi/2+\pi/3=5\pi/6\text{,}\) but the angle is clockwise, so the point on the unit circle at that angle would be
\begin{align*} \vec{u}_{R}&=\left[\begin{array}{r}\cos\left(\frac{-5\pi}{6}\right)\\[1.5ex]\sin\left(\frac{-5\pi}{6}\right)\end{array}\right]\\ &=\left[\begin{array}{r}\frac{-\sqrt{3}}{2}\\[1.5ex]\frac{-1}{2}\end{array}\right] \end{align*}
