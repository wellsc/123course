Task 4.5.1.b.

What is the value of the ratio \(\frac{OP}{OM}\text{?}\) What does this (and the fact that the triangles are similar) tell you about the ratios \(\frac{OQ}{ON}\) and \(\frac{PQ}{MN}\text{?}\)
🔗
Solution.
\begin{align*} \frac{OP}{OM}&=\frac{r}{1}\\ &=r. \end{align*}
Because corresponding sides of similar triangles have proportional lengths,
\begin{align*} \frac{OQ}{ON}&=\frac{PQ}{MN}\\ &=r. \end{align*}
