Task 4.1.2.1.c.

When the car has travelled through \(30\)m of arc length, what angle (in radians) has it travelled through?
🔗
Answer.
\(1.5\) rad.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
When when the car has travelled through \(\theta\) rad, it has travelled a distance of \(\theta\cdot 20\)m, so we can solve \(20\text{m}\cdot\theta=30\)m to get \(\theta=\frac{30\text{m}}{20\text{m}}=1.5\) rad.
 2 
It is worth noting here that if we do unit analysis of \(20\text{m}/30\text{m}\text{,}\) we see that the units cancel out. Therefore, technically radians are unitless.
