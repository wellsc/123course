Worksheet Exercise 3.

For this exercise, we will use the vector \(\BFvec{1}=\Avec{1,0}\text{.}\)
🔗

(a)

Let \(\vec{v}_{1}=\Avec{2,3}\text{.}\) Calculate \(\BFvec{1}\cdot\vec{v}_{1}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(\BFvec{1}\cdot\vec{v}_{1}=\Avec{2,3}=\vec{v}_{1}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

Let \(\vec{v}_{2}=\Avec{-5,7}\text{.}\) Calculate \(\BFvec{1}\cdot\vec{v}_{2}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(\BFvec{1}\cdot\vec{v}_{2}=\Avec{-5,7}=\vec{v}_{2}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

Let \(\vec{v}_{3}=\Avec{3,-\sqrt{2}}\text{.}\) Calculate \(\BFvec{1}\cdot\vec{v}_{3}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(\BFvec{1}\cdot\vec{v}_{3}=\Avec{2,-\sqrt{2}}=\vec{v}_{3}\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(d)

Let \(\vec{v}=\Avec{a,b}\text{,}\) where \(a\) and \(b\) are unspecified real numbers. Calculate \(\BFvec{1}\cdot\vec{v}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\(\BFvec{1}\cdot\vec{v}=\Avec{a,b}=\vec{v}\text{.}\)
