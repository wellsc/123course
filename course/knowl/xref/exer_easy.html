Worksheet Exercise 1. An Easy Problem.

A train is travelling (due east) at a speed of \(15.6\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}\text{.}\) An observer is following the front of the train with a camera from a spot \(50\)m south of the railway.
🔗
When the front of the train is at its closest point to the camera (so, due north of the camera in this case), how fast must the camera turn (in radians per second) to follow the train?
🔗
Solution.
The radius of rotation is \(50\)m, so the angular velocity is
\begin{align*} \omega &= \frac{\text{speed}}{\text{radius}}\\ &= \frac{15.6\frac{\text{m}}{\text{s}}}{50\text{m}}\\ &= 0.312\frac{\text{rad}}{\text{s}} \end{align*}
