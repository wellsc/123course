Theorem 65 The Law of Cosines.

For any triangle with angles \(A\text{,}\) \(B\text{,}\) and \(C\text{,}\) and with side lengths opposite each angle given by \(a\text{,}\) \(b\text{,}\) and \(c\text{,}\) respectively,
\begin{equation} c^{2}=a^{2}+b^{2}-2ab\cos(C).\tag{2} \end{equation}
