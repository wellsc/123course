Definition 84 The Hadamard Product.

For any \(2\)-vectors \(\vec{v}_{1}=\Avec{x_{1}, y_{1}}\) and \(\vec{v}_{2}=\Avec{x_{2}, y_{2}}\text{,}\) their Hadamard product, often written as \(\vec{v}_{1}\odot\vec{v}_{2}\text{,}\) is given by
\begin{align*} \vec{v}_{1}\odot\vec{v}_{2} &= \begin{bmatrix}x_{1}\\y_{1}\end{bmatrix} \odot \begin{bmatrix}x_{2}\\y_{2}\end{bmatrix}\\ &= \begin{bmatrix}x_{1}x_{2}\\y_{1}y_{2}\end{bmatrix} \end{align*}
🔗
More generally, for any positive integer \(n\text{,}\) for any \(n\)-vectors \(\vec{u}=\Avec{u_{1}, u_{2},\ldots,u_{n}}\) and \(\vec{v}=\Avec{v_{1}, v_{2},\ldots,v_{n}}\text{,}\) their Hadamard product, often written as \(\vec{u}\odot\vec{v}\text{,}\) is given by
\begin{align*} \vec{u}\odot\vec{v} &= \begin{bmatrix}u_{1}\\ u_{2}\\\vdots \\ u_{n}\end{bmatrix} \odot \begin{bmatrix}v_{1}\\ v_{2}\\\vdots \\ v_{n}\end{bmatrix}\\ &= \begin{bmatrix}u_{1}v_{1}\\ u_{2}v_{2}\\\vdots \\ u_{n}v_{n}\end{bmatrix} \end{align*}
